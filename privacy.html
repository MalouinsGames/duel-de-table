Privacy policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

In short

Table Duel does not collect any personal data. No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics.

Data stored on your device

To work, the game stores your coins, items, settings, statistics, records and achievements on your iPhone. This information is never sent to us.

iCloud backup

If iCloud is enabled on your device, your progress may be saved to your own iCloud account so you can find it on another device. This backup is managed by Apple and we have no access to it.

In-app purchases

The optional supporter pack is sold through the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple: we do not receive any payment information.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center on your device, the achievements you earn are sent to your Game Center profile, along with your Daily Challenge scores and solo records, which appear in Game Center leaderboards. Depending on your Game Center settings, your nickname and scores may be visible to your friends or to other players in these leaderboards. This service is run by Apple under its own privacy policy: we do not collect your nickname or your account information. You can manage your visibility or sign out of Game Center in your iPhone settings.

Children

The game does not collect any data, including from children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app erases the data stored on the device. The iCloud backup can be removed in your iPhone's iCloud settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its update date.
