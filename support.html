Support

Contact us

A problem or an idea? Write to malouinsgames@pm.me and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

How do I get the supporter pack on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Settings in the game and tap "Restore purchases".

How does the iCloud backup work?

If iCloud is enabled on your iPhone, your coins, items and statistics are saved to your account automatically.

Why can't I hear any sound?

The game respects the iPhone's silent switch. Check that switch, the volume, and the Sound setting in the game.

How do I earn coins?

Every finished game earns coins: 5 with friends, 2 solo. Finished tournaments and achievements earn coins too. Everything can be earned by playing; the supporter pack is optional.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Requests are made at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
