Support

Contact us

A problem or an idea? Write to malouinsgames@pm.me and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

How do I get the supporter pack on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Settings in the game and tap "Restore purchases".

How does the iCloud backup work?

If iCloud is enabled on your iPhone, your coins, items, statistics and achievements are saved to your account automatically.

Why can't I hear any sound?

The game respects the iPhone's silent switch. Check that switch, the volume, and the Sound setting in the game.

How do I earn coins?

Every finished game earns coins: 5 with friends, 2 solo. The Daily Challenge, finished tournaments and achievements earn coins too. Everything can be earned by playing; the supporter pack is optional.

What is the Daily Challenge?

In 1-player mode, a new little game every day, the same for everyone. Only one attempt counts for the leaderboard; after that you can practice freely. The challenge changes every day at midnight UTC.

How do I see Game Center achievements and leaderboards?

Sign in to Game Center in Settings → Game Center on your iPhone. In the game, the achievements screen then shows a "Game Center" button, and a "Leaderboard" button appears at the end of the Daily Challenge and of solo games. Game Center is optional: without it, the game keeps your achievements and records on your iPhone.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Requests are made at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
